VANE

A Uniswap v4 hook that gives a pool a posterior.

A weather vane cannot see the wind. It reveals the wind by turning.

A pool cannot see information. VANE makes it reveal information by turning: it infers the informed signal from the deflection of its own order flow and corrects its quote accordingly. No oracle, no auction, no operator.

The curve keeps settlement. A small signed offset — the belief — does discovery. When the belief is positive a buyer pays slightly more and a seller receives slightly more. A fee is unsigned and takes from both. This is a quote, not a toll.

01 / The problem

The pool under-reacts, and gets worse as it deepens

A Glosten-Milgrom market maker, after an informative buy, moves its quote past where the fill landed, because the buy was evidence. An AMM moves exactly the fill distance along the curve and stops. The gap between the mechanical move and the correct move is free money, and the next arbitrageur collects it.

The pool's log-price impact per unit of notional is set by its depth. The informationally efficient impact is set by the market: the ratio of noise volume to fundamental volatility. Setting one equal to the other gives the depth a pool would need in order to price information correctly.

2.3 — optimal depth
D* = 4U / σ
U is per-block noise notional, σ the per-block fundamental log-volatility. Depth is fixed by how much capital was deposited; these coincide only by accident.

For deep major pairs the pool is far deeper than D*, so it under-reacts. Loss-versus-rebalancing scales with the same marginal liquidity. LPs are rewarded for depositing capital, depth rises, the pool under-reacts more, informed traders accumulate more cheaply, and LVR rises. The AMM's capital-formation incentive is directly opposed to its price-discovery quality.

02 / The mechanism

Settlement stays on the curve. Discovery moves to a belief.

Effective quote
P_effective = P_curve · e^δ
Belief dynamics — 2.6
dδ = κ · dq − θ · δ · dt
Signed flow pushes the belief; θ decays it as the information becomes public elsewhere.
Correction gain — 2.5
κ = σ/(2U) − 2/D
∂κ/∂D > 0. The correction grows with depth, so the remedy scales with the disease.
Oracle-free

Every other LVR mitigation needs an exogenous price. The correction here is derived from order flow, which is what Kyle's model is for.

Signed, not a fee

With a positive belief the buyer pays more and the seller receives more. Both sides trade at a shifted price. A fee cannot do this.

Self-limiting

Flow against the belief is paid out, but that same flow drives the belief toward zero, so the exposure extinguishes itself.

04 / Estimating the inputs

Three routes, one of which is a control loop

Ticks as log prices

ln P is the tick times a constant, so a tick difference is a log return. All variance estimation is integer arithmetic: no logarithm, no exponential, no precision loss.

Route A — variance identity

In Kyle equilibrium informed and noise flow contribute equally to order-flow variance, so U is half the mean square of signed flow. Long-horizon returns supply σ; short-horizon pool volatility would understate it and fail silently.

Route B — autocovariance

Roll's trick applied to flow rather than prices. It is a test, not a primary: if A and B diverge past the bound, the pool is out of equilibrium, κ shrinks toward zero and EstimatorDivergence is emitted.

Route C — variance ratio

The pool measures whether its own price is a martingale and tunes its gain until it is. Model-free: it needs only the definition of an efficient price, not the truth of Kyle's model.

08 / Prior art

What this does not claim

Kyle-style pricing of order flow is forty years old. Data-driven market making on a curve has been studied, notably in ZeroSwap and in the adaptive-curve line of work. Loss-versus-rebalancing and the variance-ratio test are both established results. None of this is new here.

What is claimed is narrower: an on-chain implementation that matches a pool's total price impact to the Kyle impact using only its own flow, settles the correction as a signed delta inside a v4 hook, and self-calibrates its gain from its own variance ratio. The framing depends on the prior-art review, which is written before the gain library is built rather than after the results.

Lifecycle of one swap
Order arrives at the PoolManager and is routed into beforeSwap.
The scaled belief is read and clamped. No storage is written.
The offset is returned as a signed delta and settled against the reserve.
The trade settles on the curve at the shifted price.
On the next block the belief decays and the flow variance updates.
